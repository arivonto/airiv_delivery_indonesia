AIRIV Indonesia Delivery Operations

Indonesia Shipping & Logistics Operations Engine

Delivery and logistics workspace for Odoo 18 with shipment planning, carrier coordination, delivery status, Indonesian address context, and operational review surfaces.

Odoo 18 CommunityLogisticsDeliveryIndonesiaAIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV Delivery Indonesia gives teams a focused operational layer for preparing, dispatching, tracking, and reviewing shipments while remaining connected to native Odoo sales and inventory records.

Shipment Planning

Orders to dispatch

Prepare delivery work from sales and stock context with address, package, carrier, and responsibility details.

Carrier Operations

Dispatch coordination

Maintain carrier and service context, shipment references, handoff status, and delivery milestones.

Visibility

Status and review

Keep operational status, exceptions, proof-of-delivery context, and follow-up actions visible.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Configure logistics

Set warehouses, carriers, services, delivery zones, and responsible operators.

2

Prepare shipment

Select the originating order or stock operation and verify address, package, and service details.

3

Dispatch

Assign carrier, create shipment reference, record handoff, and update delivery milestones.

4

Close the loop

Review delivery status, exceptions, proof-of-delivery context, and customer follow-up.

Installation Guidance

Install

Add to Odoo addons

Clone branch 18.0, place the module on the addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list, and install.

Configure

Set delivery context

Configure warehouses, carriers, delivery services, zones, and operators.

Operate

Run shipment workflow

Prepare shipments, dispatch, update milestones, and review exceptions and proof-of-delivery.

Configuration Checklist

AddressVerify delivery address, contact, zone, and service before dispatch.
StockConfirm source operation and package availability before handoff.
CarrierAssign the correct carrier, service, and shipment reference.
ExceptionsRecord delay, failed delivery, and follow-up ownership explicitly.
